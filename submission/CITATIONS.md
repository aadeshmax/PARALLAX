## 2. Scientific Literature on Radar Inundation Physics

1. **Hess, L. L., Melack, J. M., Filoso, S., & Gastil, E. M. (1995).**
   *Delineation of inundated area and vegetation along the Amazon floodplain with the SIR-C synthetic aperture radar.*
   IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 33(4), 896-904.
   *(Foundational paper on L-band dihedral double-bounce in flooded woody biomass).*

2. **Townsend, P. A. (2001).**
   *Mapping seasonal flooding in forested wetlands using multi-temporal RADARSAT SAR.*
   International Journal of Remote Sensing, 22(5), 857-877.

3. **Martinis, S., Twele, A., & Voigt, S. (2009).**
   *Towards operational near real-time flood detection using a split-based automatic thresholding procedure on high resolution TerraSAR-X data.*
   Natural Hazards and Earth System Sciences, 9(2), 303-314.

4. **Pulvirenti, L., Chini, M., Pierdicca, N., Boni, G., & Siccardi, F. (2011).**
   *Use of SAR data for detecting floodwater in urban and agricultural areas: the role of the spatial resolution.*
   Natural Hazards and Earth System Sciences, 11(10), 2889-2902.

5. **Tsyganskaya, V., Martinis, S., Marzahn, P., & Ludwig, R. (2018).**
   *SAR-based detection of flooded vegetation—a review of characteristics and approaches.*
   International Journal of Remote Sensing, 39(8), 2255-2293.
